FAQ
When do I need to pay?
+
Temporary staff — Invoices are sent every Sunday.
Permanent staff — Salary is processed through us for the first month. Invoices are sent every Sunday.
What if I'm not happy with the staff you send?
+
Temporary staff — You may send a candidate home only if they are unable to meet your requirements or behave unacceptably (e.g. intoxicated on shift). We will arrange a replacement within 2 hours of being notified.
Permanent staff — Charges begin once the candidate passes their interview and trial shift. If they leave within 3 months, we will find a replacement at no extra charge.
Who is responsible if an accident happens at my restaurant?
+
Under UK law, you are responsible for providing first aid to the candidate with the same duty of care as your own employees. For any specific case, check here.
Are your staff experienced? How do I know they're reliable?
+
We recruit experienced staff with verified references. Some entry-level roles — such as runner and kitchen porter — are open to candidates without prior experience.
What is the minimum rate of pay?
+
UK National Minimum Wage + £2 per hour service charge.
our story

Built by people who
know hospitality.

Founded in mid-2025 by Joy Wajanavisit — UAL School President representing over 5,000 students — Finallcall was born from the cost-of-living crisis and a decade of hands-on hospitality experience across London.

Joy invited her lifelong friend Ksenia Viller, a former NHS AI/ML specialist in virology, to build the platform. Together they set out to solve hospitality's recurring staffing crisis with technology that is genuinely fair.

We share a mission: to build technology that solves hospitality's workforce challenges, keeps restaurants viable, and creates fair opportunities for every worker — regardless of background.
